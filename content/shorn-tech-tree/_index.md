An in-progress catalogue of technologies that were abandoned, foreclosed or left unbuilt. It focuses especially on still-unconquered frontiers and high-watermarks of the global technology wavefront: technologies that were not just superseded by a '[replacement technology](https://www.jstor.org/stable/43521391)', but which had something distinct that (to date) no one went on to achieve. This open [public draft](https://yuxi.ml/sketches#:~:text=use%20them%20well.-,The%20public%20draft,-is%20an%20interesting) holds my own entries and those reviewed so far: it contains {{< stt-count >}} cases, out of {{< param cases_collected >}} cases collected in total. This list likely still contains errors.
