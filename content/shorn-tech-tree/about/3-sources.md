title: "General sources used"
---
Beyond the specific linked sources for each entry, I drew on the following general channels to identify many plausible sources:

- **Books**: Vince Houghton's *[Nuking the Moon](https://www.amazon.com/Nuking-Moon-Intelligence-Schemes-Military/dp/1788163303)*, Michael Brian Schiffer's *[Spectacular Flops: Game-Changing Technologies That Failed](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctvqc6hh3)*, Vaclav Smil's *[Invention and Innovation](https://vaclavsmil.com/book/invention-and-innovation-a-brief-history-of-hype-and-failure/)*, George Basalla's *[The Evolution of Technology](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/evolution-of-technology/72D7695EE9A2BAFB75EFE648D4222A03)*; J. Storrs Hall's *[Where Is My Flying Car?](https://press.stripe.com/where-is-my-flying-car)*; Steven Novella's *[The Skeptics' Guide to the Future](https://www.amazon.com/Skeptics-Guide-Future-Yesterdays-Tomorrow/dp/1538709546)*, Schneider and Macdonald's *[The Hand Behind Unmanned](https://academic.oup.com/book/58870)*; and various project- or industry-specific books, e.g. Samuel Benjamin's *[Spaceships Lost in Time](https://www.amazon.ca/Spaceships-Lost-Time-Cancelled-Spacecraft/dp/B096TK5MC5)*; Tony Buttler's *[American Secret Projects](https://www.amazon.com/American-Secret-Projects-Submarine-Aircraft/dp/191080990X)*; Scott Lowther's *[US Supersonic Bomber Projects](https://www.amazon.com/US-Supersonic-Bomber-Projects-Vol/dp/1911703188/)*; George Dyson's *[Project Orion](https://www.amazon.com/Project-Orion-Story-Atomic-Spaceship/dp/0805059857)*, Bruce Franklin's *[War Stars](https://www.amazon.com/War-Stars-Superweapon-American-Imagination/dp/0195052951)*
- **Journal articles**: collected entries of the [1989 ICOHTEC Symposium on Failed Innovations](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3106066); selected back issue articles of *Vulcan*, especially its 2019 *[Special Issue on Technological Determinism in War](https://brill.com/view/journals/vulc/7/1/vulc.7.issue-1.xml)*; selected *Technology and Culture* journal articles
- **Blogs**: AI Impacts' '[incentivized technologies not pursued](https://wiki.aiimpacts.org/responses_to_ai/technological_inevitability/incentivized_technologies_not_pursued/start)' and '[resisted technological temptations](https://wiki.aiimpacts.org/responses_to_ai/technological_inevitability/incentivized_technologies_not_pursued/resisted_technological_temptations_project)' [projects](https://aiimpacts.org/what-weve-learned-so-far-from-our-technological-temptations-project/); [Aerospace Projects Review](https://www.aerospaceprojectsreview.com/blog/); [Incautious Optimism](https://jordanwtaylor2.substack.com/); [Secret Projects Forum](https://www.secretprojects.co.uk/#secret-unbuilt-projects.3); [Museum of Failure](https://museumoffailure.com/#explore); [Old Machine Press](https://oldmachinepress.com/), [Creatures of Thought](https://technicshistory.com/about/), [Abort Retry Fail](https://www.abortretry.fail/archive), [Space Flight History](https://spaceflighthistory.blogspot.com/)
- **YouTube channels**: [Mustard](https://www.youtube.com/@mustardchannel); [Found and Explained](https://www.youtube.com/foundandexplained), [Retro Transport](https://www.youtube.com/@RetroTransport)
- **AI:** I also used AI (ChatGPT, Claude) to identify candidate cases across industries. In all these cases, before adding them into the long-list for inclusion, all cases were given a basic manual review and check, ensuring I could trace the claim or case to a distinct source; with around a quarter of proposed cases discarded.
