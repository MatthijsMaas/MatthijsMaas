{{< marginnote >}}<img src="/img/portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Matthijs Maas">{{< /marginnote >}}I am a Senior Research Fellow at the Institute for Law & AI; an Associate Fellow at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence (University of Cambridge), and a non-residential research associate at King’s College, University of Cambridge.

I am the author of [*Architectures of Global AI Governance: From Technological Change to Human Choice*](/publication/2025.-maas-architectures-of-global-ai-governance/) (OUP 2025).{{< sidenote >}}Available as an [open access digital version](https://academic.oup.com/book/61416) and as a [print book](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/architectures-of-global-ai-governance-9780198877837).{{< /sidenote >}}

My work focuses on mapping theories of change for advanced AI governance, international institutional designs for AI, the effect of AI on international law, and arms control regimes for military uses of AI technology.{{< marginnote >}}PhD in Law, University of Copenhagen, 2021. MSc International Relations, University of Edinburgh, 2013.{{< /marginnote >}}

With José Jaime Villalobos and Christoph Winter, I am the author of *International Catastrophe Law: From Nuclear Weapons to Advanced AI* (Cambridge University Press, 2027 forthcoming).{{< sidenote >}}The [introduction](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6764898) and [first chapter](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7033960) are on SSRN.{{< /sidenote >}}

I write the blog [*Critical Maas*](https://criticalmaas.substack.com/), on Substack.{{< marginnote >}}Elsewhere: [email](mailto:mmm71@cantab.ac.uk), [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Fe64DJQAAAAJ&hl=en), [X](https://x.com/matthijsMmaas), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matthijsmaas/), [GitHub](https://github.com/MatthijsMaas).{{< /marginnote >}}
