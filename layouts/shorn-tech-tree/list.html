  • 27 November 2026, fourth update: in the ‘development hell’ glyph every earlier attempt now ends in a ‘terminated’ mark; the latest attempt is still under way.

  • 27 November 2026, second update: a dot marks cases added or revised since your last visit (or in the last 14 days, on a first visit), with a filter; a tick in each entry’s bottom line marks the entries I have reviewed, with a filter; a key to both above the list.
  • 27 November 2026: seven entries revised (STT-0011 Wunderwaffe, STT-0019 NEOM ‘The Line’, STT-0025 aerial relay system, STT-0031 rail-launched heavy bombers, STT-0086 US space stations, STT-0102 micro-space stations, STT-0329 Panama Canal); STT-0482 (US nuclear spacecraft propulsion) split into STT-0499 (nuclear thermal) and STT-0500 (nuclear-electric). 499 cases.
  • 25 November 2026, third update: “Lost ‘dark matter’ innovations” renamed “Depressed innovation fields”; the introduction counts the technologies abandoned or never (yet) realised; a new glyph for Procurement and deployment bungles (collapse at roll-out, while a replacement carries on); a light/dark switch in the corner bar; readable dropdown lists in dark mode.
  • 25 November 2026, second update: in the key, ‘active pursuit’ becomes ‘pursuit decision’, so it does not read as ‘still being pursued’.
  • 25 November 2026: a new category in the soil of the tree, Overhanging fruit (ideas that took surprisingly long to arise), with its definition; no cases in it yet.
  • 24 November 2026, second update: three cases added (STT-0496 small US Army nuclear reactors, STT-0497 tilting high-speed train, STT-0498 ‘Terminator’ sterile seeds); STT-0045 (military moon bases) corrected; nine entries marked ‘(arguably)’; a source restored on STT-0063. 498 cases.

  • 22 November 2026: corrections to three entries (STT-0100, STT-0336, STT-0337); British spelling throughout, except in quotations and names.
